## Saint Dominic's International School (2022 - Present)

I joined Saint Dominic's International School in August 2022, and have been teaching mathematics within the International Baccalaureate (IB) framework since then. In this school, I have held teaching roles with the added responsibility of being a form tutor during the 2023-2024 school year.

### Courses taught
1. IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) Mathematics:
    - MYP 2, MYP 3, MYP 4 (Standard and Extended) and MYP 5 (Standard and Extended)
2. IB Diploma Programme (DP) Mathematics 
    - Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (SL and HL)
