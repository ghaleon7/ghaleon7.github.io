## What can you expect from me as your tutor?

As your tutor, my goal is to help you understand mathematics, prepare for examinations and tackle challenging problems. 
I offer in-person and online sessions, adapting each lesson to your goals and needs. 

For online lessons, I am familiar with platforms such as Google Meet, Discord and Zoom, use digital tools such as GeoGebra, Desmos to support explanations and visualizations. After each lesson, I provide the lesson notes so you can review them later.
